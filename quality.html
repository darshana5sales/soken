Quality without compromise.

Quality is not a check at the end of the line. It is a set of documented decisions taken at every point before it.

Standards We Work To

Certified, and verifiable.

Certificates are available to partners on request. Our documentation travels with the product.

01
WHO-GMP
Good Manufacturing Practice, to WHO guidelines

Our facility is operated and audited against the WHO's Good Manufacturing Practice guidelines, covering premises, personnel, process control and batch release — the reference standard for pharmaceutical and nutraceutical manufacturing worldwide.

02
FSSAI
Food Safety & Standards Authority of India

Compliance with India's food safety framework across ingredient permissibility, label accuracy, additive limits and manufacturing hygiene — the mandatory basis for any consumer health product sold in India.

03
ISO 9001
Quality Management Systems

Our quality management system is certified to ISO 9001, which governs process consistency, documentation control and continuous improvement across the whole operation, not only the production floor.

Quality Control

Every stage. Every batch.

A finished product carries the accumulated decisions of everyone who touched it. So we test at the points where a problem can still be caught cheaply, rather than discovering it at release when the only option left is to destroy the batch.

By the time a unit leaves the facility it has been checked five separate times, by five separate criteria, and the record of each check stays with its lot number permanently.

Request Our Documentation →
01

Raw material qualification

Incoming materials tested for identity, purity, potency and contamination before acceptance. Only vendors who have passed qualification and can document provenance are used.

02

In-process controls

Blend uniformity, weight variation, hardness and dissolution monitored at defined intervals through the run, with microbial controls throughout.

03

Finished product analysis

HPLC assay for active content, dissolution testing, microbial limits, heavy metal screening, and full specification compliance before anything is packed.

04

Packaging & stability

Pack integrity verification, plus accelerated and real-time stability studies that establish shelf life under the storage conditions printed on the label.

05

Batch release

Formal review and sign-off by qualified personnel, with complete batch records, certificate of analysis and regulatory documentation, before a single unit leaves the site.

Manufacturing Standards

Built on a foundation of rigour.

01

Clean room facility

Controlled environment production with defined air classifications and contamination prevention protocols.

02

SOP-driven operations

Every process governed by a written Standard Operating Procedure, with deviations recorded and investigated.

03

Qualified personnel

Trained quality professionals and regulatory specialists accountable for compliance at each operational level.

04

Batch traceability

Complete records from raw material lot to finished unit, traceable in both directions for every batch produced.

Our Commitment

The Soken quality promise.

01

Ingredient integrity

Every ingredient comes from a qualified supplier with full documentation. We test what we say we use, and we use what we say we test.

02

Label accuracy

What the label declares is what the product contains, verified by analytical testing for active content within approved tolerance limits.

03

Safety first

Microbial testing, heavy metal screening and contaminant analysis are performed on every batch, without exception and without shortcuts.
